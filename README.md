## How to Choose the Right Wallet for You
* Carry little, value slimness? Choose a bifold.
* Carry lots of cards and receipts? Choose a trifold.
* Prefer bags or suit jackets, and want bills kept flat? Choose a long wallet.

Whichever you choose, check the leather type, the stitching and the edge finish. Those details decide how long a wallet lasts. For background on how leather is produced responsibly, the [Leather Working Group](https://www.leatherworkinggroup.com/) is a good place to start.
